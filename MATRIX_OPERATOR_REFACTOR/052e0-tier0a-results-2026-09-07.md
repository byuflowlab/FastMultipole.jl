## Scope and kernel under test

Tier 0A exercises the **low-level kernel**
`pnl._induced(..., ConstantDoublet, ...)`
(`FLOWPanel_elements_fmm.jl:729-838`) — the same routine
`HybridWakePotential` reaches for wake-panel potential via
`solve_formulation!` → `_wake_potential!` → `influence!` →
`FastMultipole.direct!` → `induced` (call chain verified by reading; wake
panels of a lifting body use pure `ConstantDoublet`,
`FLOWPanel_elements_fmm.jl:1452`). Gate A10 additionally verifies parity of
the public `pnl.induced(target, body, i)` wrapper against the low-level
routine on one panel; the full `PanelWake → influence! → direct!` route
(buffer packing, quad splitting) is NOT executed here and is exercised in
Tiers 0B/1.

## Identified convention

$$\varphi_{\rm impl} = -\frac{\mu\,\Omega}{4\pi}$$ exactly (sign $s=+1$,
branch constants $c_\pm=0$: the per-panel zero-at-infinity branch), for
panels wound CCW seen from the $+\hat n$ side; upper-minus-lower jump
$[\varphi]=-\mu$; loop circulation $\Gamma=+\mu$ for a loop positively
linking $+\hat n$. This is the plan's stated convention.

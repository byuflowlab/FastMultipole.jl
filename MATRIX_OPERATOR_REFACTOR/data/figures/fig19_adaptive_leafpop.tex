% fig19 (041a): leaf-population complementary CDF (fraction of leaves with
% population >= x), multiscale field at contrast 100 and 1000, n = 1e6 —
% the mechanistic explanation of the timing gap.  The uniform grid at any
% fixed depth leaves a fat tail: at the auto-scale depth ell = 5 the densest
% cells reach popmax in the thousands, and each such cell serializes O(K^2)
% direct nearfield work; deeper depths shrink the tail but multiply cells
% and routes.  The adaptive octree truncates the distribution at exactly
% K_max = 64 by construction (vertical cutoff).
